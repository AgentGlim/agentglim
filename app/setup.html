Harnesses

The coding agents you use. Turn off the ones you don't: their cards, provider rows and limit bars are hidden on the dashboard.

Jev routing

Free APIs and accounts

Free tiers the worker lanes can use. Sign up, then paste the key into your .env. Key values are never shown here: only whether one is set.

Paid APIs

Voice, sound effects and music. Owner sessions only: plan runs never use these keys.

Custom projects

Your own projects, shown as cards in the dashboard's Custom projects section. Point at a folder (file and folder counts, last change) or a data file (.json items, done/total progress, row counts for .csv/.ndjson). Read-only.

Local AI

Local models, the plans folder and notifications. All optional. Dependency report · Installed skills
