# Research policy

## What to watch

Start with primary-source release feeds. Record the source URL, publication time, goal, the
specific change and a practical next step.

## What not to do

Do not treat a release announcement as a measured capability. Do not run automatic upgrades, model
loads or paid tool trials.

## Experiments

Recommend at most one new experiment a week, with a time cap, success metric, stop condition and
rollback. Keep private client facts in the project that owns them.
