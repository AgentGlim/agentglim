# Operating policy

## Priority and capacity

Protect existing paying client delivery first. After those commitments, the example goals split the
remaining time 60% Ship and 40% Learn. These are recommended shares, not measured hours.

Keep at most two active growth outcomes. Support work must unblock one of them or produce a measured
cost saving.

## Correct drift

A task is directly aligned when it delivers a current client commitment or a named acceptance gate.
Otherwise park it with a short reason and offer a smaller aligned next action.

Missing activity data means unknown, not drift. Count commits and agent jobs as attention proxies
only.

## Weekly rhythm

Monday: refresh evidence, compare outcomes and activity, pick the next steps and at most one
experiment. Friday: record outcomes and buyer signals. A review should take about 30 minutes.
